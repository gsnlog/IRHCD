Three Conditions for Transition Length

Compare All Train Types

Rate of Cant varies by train type:
  • Passenger: 35 mm/sec (standard for mail/express)
  • Goods: 25 mm/sec (slower to prevent load shifting)
  • Suburban: 55 mm/sec (faster rate acceptable for EMUs)
