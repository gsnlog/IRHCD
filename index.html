    User Guide

    Getting Started

    Welcome to Curve Designer. This tool helps calculate railway curve specifications based on Indian Railway standards.

    Design New Curve

    1. Enter curve parameters (radius, chord length, speeds)
    2. Configure track standard and route group
    3. Click Generate Report to calculate
    4. Review compliance notes and step-by-step calculations
    5. Export or print the report as needed

    Check Existing Curve

    1. Switch to "Check Existing Curve" tab
    2. Enter measured field data (radius, cant, transition)
    3. Click Check Curve to verify compliance
    4. Review findings and recommendations

    Key Features

    • Ribbon Interface: Quick access to common actions
    • Step-by-Step Calculation: See detailed math for every result
    • Compliance Checking: Automatic validation against standards
    • Export/Import: Save and load your project data
    • Light/Dark Theme: Toggle appearance preference

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is versine?

    Versine is the perpendicular distance from the chord to the curve arc. It's calculated as V = C² / (8R) × 1000 in millimeters.

    What is cant?

    Cant is the banking of the track on curves, measured in millimeters. It reduces lateral forces on trains and improves ride quality.

    What is cant deficiency?

    Cant deficiency is the additional theoretical cant needed to maintain perfect speed without wheel climb or slip. Governed by track standard limits.

    How is transition length calculated?

    Transition length is the maximum of three criteria: cant rate (0.0056 or 0.008 × cant × speed), deficiency rate, and cant gradient (0.72 × cant).

    What does "Non-transitioned with Virtual Transition" mean?

    Non-transitioned curves are followed by a straight section. A virtual transition is applied mathematically for design and checking purposes.

    Can I export my data?

    Yes. Click the Export button (💾) in the header or ribbon to download your design data as JSON. Use Import to restore it later.

    How do I print a report?

    Click Report in the ribbon to preview the formatted report, then choose Print or Save as PDF.

    What standards are supported?

    This tool supports Indian Railway standards including BG (Broad Gauge) with various cant and cant deficiency limits.
